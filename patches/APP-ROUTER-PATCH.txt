APP ROUTER PATCH — src/App.jsx

const Subscription = lazy(() => import("./pages/Subscription"));

<Route
  path="/dashboard/subscription"
  element={
    <ProtectedPage>
      <Subscription />
    </ProtectedPage>
  }
/>

Do not replace the entire App.jsx file. Insert only these lines.
